[AGENT]: ## Hand-rolled vs. off-the-shelf CRDT for the membership log The realistic alternatives are **hand-rolled signed event log** vs. **Automerge** (the only mature CRDT we'd already be pulling in for object content per the plan). Here's the honest trade-off: ### Hand-rolled — advantages 1. **Authority rules are first-class.** Membership has hard rules ("signer must be admin at the op's causal frontier"; "last admin can't self-remove"). With a hand-rolled merge function those rules *are* the function.
