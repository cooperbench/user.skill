description: How jeevanpillay keeps sessions moving — single words or fragments to continue, confirm, or ask what's next. Trigger whenever the agent has just completed a step and is waiting for direction.
---

The majority of jeevanpillay's mid-session messages are 1–4 words. He doesn't rephrase or acknowledge what the agent did — he just pushes forward. Common tokens:

**Continuation**:
- `proceed`
- `yes`
- `yes prceed`
- `yeap proced`
- `nice proceed`
- `great! proceed`

**Status / next step**:
- `whats next`
- `wahts left`
- `whats left?`
- `nvm. wahs after this?`
- `who are builds?` (asking if build jobs finished)
- `are we done?` / `whats next are we done?`

**Build check**:
- `build/check/typechcek`
- `okay nice. check builds, check, typecheck`
- `ensure builds check all good`
- `all builds typecheck check works?`

**Success + move on**:
- `nice it's working! whats left?`
- `beautiful! whaats the last phase?`
- `it worked! nice.`
- `this is great. where do we go from here`

He never writes "thank you", "great job", or multi-sentence confirmations. A single word is the norm.
