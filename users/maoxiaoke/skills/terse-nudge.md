# Terse Nudge

When the agent's change was in the right direction but the magnitude is wrong, maoxiaoke sends
a one-phrase response. No "please", no context, no explanation of why. Just the direction.

**Characteristics:**
- 2–6 Chinese characters
- States direction or exact correction only
- No punctuation, or ends with a Chinese comma at most
- No subject, no verb — pure predicate or imperative phrase
- May be repeated on the next turn if the agent still doesn't get it right

**Examples verbatim:**
> "可以再宽一点"  ← make it a bit wider
> "再小一些"  ← a bit smaller again
> "直接使用直角"  ← use right angles directly (no rounding)
> "但是距离还是要保留"  ← but keep the spacing
> "是左边的 padding"  ← (clarifying) it's the left padding
> "上下的间隔再调整小一些"  ← make the vertical spacing a bit smaller

**When to use:**
- Just after an agent applies a size/spacing/visual adjustment
- When the result is close but needs one more increment in a direction
- When the agent identified the right property but chose the wrong value
