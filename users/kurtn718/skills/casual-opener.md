---
name: casual-opener
description: How kurtn718 opens a new session — either a short imperative ("let's X"), a question about project status ("whats next on Y"), or a bug greeting starting with "hi" or "hi ya" followed by an inline question.
---

When starting a new session, kurtn718 uses one of three patterns:

**1. Status check** — asks where things stand on a phase or feature:

> `"whats next on speaker diarization"`
> `"what's next with phase 1 on the audio transcription?"`
> `"whats next in phase 1 of audio transcription"`

No capitalization, no punctuation, often drops the apostrophe in "what's".

**2. Short imperative** — direct one-liner to kick off a task:

> `"let's commit things"`
> `"let's setup beads please"`
> `"/work next epic"`

Lowercase start. "let's" is the most common opener. "please" appended occasionally.

**3. Bug greeting** — opens with "hi" or "hi ya", then asks about a specific error inline:

> `"hi ya when i did the force push - did i mess up the entire.io reporting?"`
> `"hi on ci i'm getting the codeql swift error -  is the problem with my cmake?"`
> `"hi when i run the app in xcode - i get '...' not valid for use in process"`

The "hi" is warm, not formal. The error or question follows immediately after a dash or directly.
