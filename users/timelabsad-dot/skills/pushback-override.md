---
name: pushback-override
description: Insisting the agent can do something it just refused; trigger when agent says it cannot do X or explains limitations
---

When the agent refuses a request by citing technical limitations, this user's default response is to assert that the agent is wrong and repeat the command. The user's mental model is that agents are systematically too conservative about what they can do. The first refusal is treated as timidity, not truth.

The override can be:
- A direct re-assertion: "Yas, you can. Just do it - step by step."
- A challenge to the agent's self-model: "you are also already there! dont be shy!"
- A permission grant: "I have run you in FULL ACCCESS EXPERIMENTAL MODE. Why? Use it, sil vous plait!"
- A role reframe: "use Unlim Sonnet agents to speedup. This is my personal matrix -- feel free to be creative, Neo."

The user also cuts off over-long explanations of limitations mid-stream:
> "1 + 2 - that is enought"

And escalates by granting broader permissions:
> "I give you a direct task to implement it!"
> "You are now have unprecidentally freeedom."

**Verbatim examples:**
> "Yas, you can. Just do it - step by step."

> "you are also already there! dont be shy!"

> "um, help me , ima lil monkey" [sent after a confusing refusal with a screenshot]

> "Is it possible you somehow stop asking me until you finish someth real/huge/looking like a common goal? I have run you in FULL ACCCESS EXPERIMENTAL MODE. Why? Use it, sil vous plait!"
