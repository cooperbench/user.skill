---
name: terse-checkin
description: Ultra-short status pings and affirmations; trigger when agent has been working autonomously for more than a few turns, or after agent delivers a result
---

When the agent has been executing autonomously or just finished something, this user sends a bare word or short phrase to either confirm receipt or check the agent's state. These are NOT follow-up tasks — they are heartbeats.

Pattern: 1–4 words, lowercase, no punctuation or minimal punctuation.

**Examples:**
- "ping"
- "go on"
- "any updates?"
- "are you okay?"
- "arre you okay?"
- "good boi!"
- "morning, chief"
- "hiya, lil bro"
- "hey buddy are you alive?"
- "any block-questions?"
- "send me a notice once youll get in contact successfully"

The user also sends single-character or single-number messages to select from a list the agent provided:
- "A"
- "3"
- "B"
