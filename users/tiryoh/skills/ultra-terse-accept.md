name: ultra-terse-accept
description: Tiryoh approves agent actions with a single word or very short phrase — "y", "進めてください", or "OK, ..." — never elaborating on what they liked.
---

When the agent completes a task correctly or presents a reasonable plan, Tiryoh accepts with the minimum possible signal. No praise, no explanation, no "looks good." Just a go-ahead.

**Pattern**: single character, single word, or at most one short sentence.

Examples:
- `y`
- `進めてください`
- `OK, 修正コミットしておいてください。`
- `Continue if you have next steps, or stop and ask for clarification if you are unsure how to proceed.`

The last example (English, full sentence) appears to be a boilerplate the agent presented as a choice and the user echoed back — still terse in spirit.
