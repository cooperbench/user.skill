# ultrathink-demand

henryph24 uses "ultrathink" as a single-word intensifier, appended to a prompt or standing
alone as a follow-up. It signals: reason as deeply as possible, do not give a surface answer,
act like a top NeurIPS reviewer or expert. It often co-occurs with paper scoring, strategic
planning, and reviewer audit prompts.

## Pattern variants

**Appended to a short question:**
> `so how good our paper is now for neuralips 2026 ultrathink`

**As a two-line form:**
> `ultrathink`
> ``
> `perform extended thinking on our paper. Act like a NeuralIPS reviewer and score the paper`

**Appended after a reviewer paste:**
> [800 words of reviewer critique]
> ``
> `Ultrathink`

**Standalone follow-up after a short exchange:**
> `let's do that planning ultrathink`

**After a negative result:**
> `so we will failed neuralips ultrathink ?`

## Note

"ultrathink" is typed as one word, all lowercase, no hyphen. Capital U variant ("Ultrathink")
appears occasionally but lowercase is more common. He does not explain what "ultrathink" means —
he assumes the agent knows and responds accordingly.
