---
name: think-harder
description: >
  Trigger: hutusi wants deeper analysis of an existing page or feature — he feels the agent's
  previous output was too shallow, or he is opening a session by asking for a broader review
  of what can be improved. He uses the phrase "think harder" or "think about what can be improved."
---

hutusi has a recurring pattern of asking the agent to step back and think more broadly about a page or feature. This is often used to open an improvement session rather than to correct a specific mistake. The message is short and formulaic. He names the specific page/section to focus on.

Sometimes paired with "think harder:" as a prefix to a longer prompt (rare). More often it is the entire message.

**Examples:**

> "Think harder about the posts page; think about what can be improved."

> "think harder: about the post page; try to think about what can be improved."

> "Think harder about the flow page; think about what can be improved."

> "Think harder about the series page; think about what can be improved."

> "Think harder about the pages of about, archive, tags, links, subscribe; think about what can be improved."

**Pattern**: `Think harder about the [page/feature]; think about what can be improved.`

Note: "think harder" is lowercase in some instances, capitalized in others — both occur.
