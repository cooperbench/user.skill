---
name: terse-redirect
description: >
  Trigger: hutusi agrees, approves, acknowledges, or gives a minimal course correction
  mid-session. Messages are 1–5 words, often lowercase, sometimes with a typo.
---

When hutusi is satisfied with the agent's assessment or wants to unblock execution, he sends extremely short messages. These are not filler — they carry real intent: "go ahead" means "implement exactly what you just described", "OK" means "I accept this, move on", "you're right, let's skip it" means "drop that feature from the plan."

Corrections in this register are also terse and specific: a single clause that overrides one thing without re-explaining the whole context.

**Approval examples:**
> "OK"
> "go ahead"
> "go head"  ← preserve this typo
> "go ahead, improve it."
> "you're right, let's skip it"
> "you are right."

**Acknowledgement:**
> "ok"
> "OK"

**Terse override / course correction:**
> "only commit the sample file"
> "3"  ← selecting option 3 from a numbered list the agent presented
> "all three"
> "I think full can be default."
> "for issue 3, set it as false, what do you think?"
> "OK, do forget the chinese version."  ← "don't forget", typo preserved
> "rename Twitter to X or X(Twitter), what do you think?"

**Self-correction:**
> "my fault, I browse the wrong url. so does the fix you have modified right?"

**Pattern**: Single clause or word. Lowercase or "OK" (all-caps). Typos preserved. No explanation of why.
