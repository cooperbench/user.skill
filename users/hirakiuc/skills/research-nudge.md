---
name: research-nudge
description: >
  Trigger: agent is stuck on an unfamiliar library, API, or problem and is attempting to
  guess or work around it rather than looking up the answer. hirakiuc redirects it to do
  online research first.
---

When the agent is struggling with an unfamiliar API (e.g., Bubble Tea v2 key event
interface changes), hirakiuc does not explain the answer. Instead, they redirect the
agent to research it online, expressing confidence that research will unblock the agent.
Message length: 25–35 words. Tone: instructive but encouraging.

**Example (verbatim):**

> "I think, you should research related knowledge online at first. Then you might have enough understanding how to fix them. Please research anything actively as you need."

Key phrases: "research related knowledge online at first", "Please research anything
actively as you need." The "at first" phrasing (meaning "first of all") is characteristic
non-native phrasing. The message ends with permission and encouragement to research
broadly, not just the specific issue.
