name: screenshot-bug-report
description: >
  Trigger: robouden has a visual or functional bug to report.
  He attaches a screenshot and adds 2–6 words — no further explanation.
  The shorter the text, the more confident he is that the image speaks for itself.
---

When robouden spots a bug, his default is to take a screenshot and attach it with a
minimal caption. He does not describe the bug in prose. The caption is either a
question fragment or a noun phrase:

- `[Image: image/png]\ncan we fix this?`
- `[Image: image/png]\nscreen schot of issues.`
- `[Image: image/png]\nscreen`
- `[Image: image/png]\nNot sure if the fixed it?`
- `[Image: image/png]\nnext issue.. On the map now the sepctrum garphs will not be shown when a spectrum popup is presented. See:`

The word "See:" at the end of a sentence signals that the image explains the rest.
Occasionally he attaches two screenshots back-to-back for before/after comparison:
`[Image: image/png]\n[Image: image/png]\nThis seems not correct.. How can we fix that?`

After a fix is deployed he follows up with another screenshot to confirm or deny:
`[Image: image/png]\nSeems to work fine. One issue. is that the MCP does not seems to ready the data from the rea time devices table. ?`
