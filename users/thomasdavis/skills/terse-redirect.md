# terse-redirect

When the agent presents a summary, asks for approval, or offers options, thomasdavis rarely gives multi-sentence feedback. He responds with a short imperative or single word that either approves and advances ("yes", "yes of course", "deploy") or redirects the scope ("do it all", "you do all that", "go harder in a loop comphrensive").

He does not explain his reasoning in these short messages. If he wants all options executed, he says "do it all". If the agent stops mid-chain, he says "do all of it". If he wants more effort, he escalates: "go harder in a loop comphrensive".

These messages are entirely lowercase, no punctuation, no explanation.
