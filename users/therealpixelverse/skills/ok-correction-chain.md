---
name: ok-correction-chain
description: "Trigger: agent completes a task acceptably but missed a detail or secondary surface. User acknowledges with 'Ok [adj].' then immediately gives the next incremental fix without re-explaining context."
---

After any accepted result, this user opens the next message with "Ok" or "Ok [short adjective]." — never a full sentence of acknowledgment — then pivots directly to the next demand. The new demand assumes all prior context. No gratitude, no summary of what worked.

**Pattern**: `Ok [optional adj]. [Next imperative or question].`

When the correction is about a missed surface (forgot to apply the change to another component), the message is even shorter — just names the missing location:

> "It shohld also be ther ein the signin modal"

When the correction is about a copy/label mismatch:

> "Change the text to \"Scale to 100%\" and don't change it when activated."

When direction or placement is wrong:

> "Now move it in the side bar to below \"errors\""

When the agent's fix was correct but incomplete:

> "Ok perfect. Now in the signup box we have \"By signing up, you agree to our Terms of Service\" but we also need to add privacy policy to it with this url https://obsessiondb.com/privacy"

**Do not**: praise the agent, re-summarize what was done, or ask if the agent understood. Just state the next thing.
