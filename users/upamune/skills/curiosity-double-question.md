description: >
  Trigger: upamune is genuinely curious about how something works in his own project.
  He asks a short Japanese question with `??` and sometimes a "すごい！" opener.
---

upamune uses `??` (double question mark) instead of `?` when asking casual understanding questions. These are the only messages where he shows enthusiasm—often opening with "すごい！" (amazing!). The questions are about the project's own design or behavior, not about external technology.

He sometimes answers part of his own question to confirm he's on the right track: "〜みたいな感じかな?" (something like...?). He expects the agent to confirm or correct.

**Examples:**

Enthusiastic discovery:
> "すごい！これagent でファイルの書き込みとか編集したやつを実際に反映したいときはどうすればいいの??"

Self-answering follow-up:
> "1 はどんな感じになる?? Ctrl-D で終了した時にこのセッションの変更をapply するためのコマンドが表示されてる感じかな?"

Practical verification question:
> ".github/workflows/release.yml 出やってる bun の compile は手元でも通る??"

Compile question (single `?` variant—shorter, more direct):
> "compile するには？"
