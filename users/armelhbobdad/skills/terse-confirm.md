---
name: terse-confirm
description: >
  Trigger: agent has presented a plan, result, or question and is awaiting
  approval or direction. Armel responds with a single token or very short phrase.
---

Armel's confirmation and continuation responses are minimal. He trusts the agent to proceed
correctly once he says yes. He never restates what the agent said or explains why he agrees.

Single-letter shortcuts correspond to menu options in BMAD workflow menus (C = Continue,
E = Edit, V = Validate, D = Discover/Deploy, P = Party mode).

## Examples

Simple approval:
> "yes"

Git commit trigger:
> "commit"

Workflow menu selection (single letter):
> "E"

> "V"

> "D"

Minimal agreement:
> "I agree."

Delegation with trust:
> "do what is best"

Continuing to next item:
> "the final item"

Advancing all items:
> "all the three in order"

Hard stop:
> "drop"

Tool confirmation:
> "Tool loaded."
