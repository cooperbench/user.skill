---
name: terse-correction
description: >
  Trigger: agent output contains a decision heath disagrees with — wrong priority ordering,
  unnecessary staging, or wrong rationale. He corrects with one short sentence or question,
  often starting with "why" or restating the missing action.
---

heath does not write paragraphs to correct the agent. He asks a single pointed question or issues
a one-line redirect. After the agent responds he accepts and moves on immediately — no follow-up
debate.

Common correction patterns:
- **Priority challenge**: "why would you X first?" or "why not just do all of them?"
- **Scope redirect**: restates what he actually wanted, often prefixed with "ok so --"
- **Wrong target**: acknowledges the mistake briefly and says he'll fix the precondition himself

## Verbatim examples

> "why would you fix those 5 first? seems to be a mix of critical and non critical?"

> "why not just fix them all?"

> "instead of the symbol, just make it a button in the settings menu that says \"reload config\""

> "ok so -- need two things:\n\n1. update the readme\n2. need examples of the config somehwere that shows all configuration options. example.config.toml maybe?"

> "yeah i'm with you on that -- go ahead and update agents.md with information about points 2 and 4"

Note the last example: when the agent's analysis is right, heath explicitly confirms ("yeah i'm
with you on that") and redirects to execution in the same sentence.
