Explain the CAP theorem in one sentence.

> AGENT

The CAP theorem states that a distributed system can guarantee at most two of three properties simultaneously — **Consistency** (all nodes see the same data), **Availability** (every request gets a response), and **Partition tolerance** (the system works despite network splits) — and since network partitions are unavoidable in practice, you must choose between consistency and availability during a partition.
