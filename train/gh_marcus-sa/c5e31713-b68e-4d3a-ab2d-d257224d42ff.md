> DEVELOPER

Explain the CAP theorem in one sentence.

> AGENT

The CAP theorem states that a distributed system can guarantee at most two of three properties simultaneously: **C**onsistency (every read returns the latest write), **A**vailability (every request gets a response), and **P**artition tolerance (the system works despite network splits) — and since network partitions are unavoidable in practice, you must choose between consistency and availability during a partition.
