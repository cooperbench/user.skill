4. **Knowledge graph guides specifically** recommend "color edges by predicate family" — exactly what your current `edgeStyle()` does.

5. **Your current multi-channel encoding is best practice** — color for type, dash pattern for category, opacity for hierarchy.

**If you still want the "flow" aesthetic**, hybrid alternatives exist:
- Gradient edges (relationship color → target color)
- Target-colored arrowheads only (edge body keeps relationship color)
