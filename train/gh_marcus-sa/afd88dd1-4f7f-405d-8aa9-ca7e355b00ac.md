oh gpt-5 mini has a reasoning model but gpt-4.1-mini does not?

> AGENT

Correct. OpenAI's model families split differently: - **gpt-4.1 family** (gpt-4.1, gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-4.1-nano) — traditional completion models. For your extraction use case — structured JSON output with deterministic results — gpt-4.1-mini is the right model class. Reasoning is wasted overhead for "parse this text and fill in a schema."
