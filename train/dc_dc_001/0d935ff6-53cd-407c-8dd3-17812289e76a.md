**NMSL** is a Chinese internet slang acronym that stands for **"你妈死了"** (nǐ mā sǐ le), which translates to "your mom is dead." It's a highly offensive and vulgar insult used in Chinese online communities, particularly in flame wars and toxic comment sections.

It gained widespread notoriety around 2019-2020 during online conflicts between Chinese and Thai internet users on platforms like Twitter and Instagram.

It's considered extremely rude and is not something to use in any civil conversation.
