15 MINUTE is the best? really?

> AGENT

Yes, unfortunately. Android's `PeriodicWorkRequest` enforces a **hard minimum of 15 minutes** — this is baked into the OS, not a library choice. Calling it with anything less silently clamps to 15. That said, in practice the actual interval is often **longer** than 15 minutes due to: - **Doze mode** — delays background work when the device is idle - **App Standby Buckets** — if the user rarely opens the app, Android throttles further (up to hours) - **Battery Saver** — can defer all background work This is exactly why we added the **foreground coroutine poller at 1 minute** — when the user actually has the app open, they get near-real-time updates. The 15-minute WorkManager poll is just a fallback for when the app is closed. The only way to get faster background notifications would be a push-based approach (FCM/UnifiedPush), which requires backend WebPush support that Hackers' Pub doesn't have yet.
