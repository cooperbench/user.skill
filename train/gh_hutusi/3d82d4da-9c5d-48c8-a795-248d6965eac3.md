Here's how enchanting works in Monecraft. There's no separate "enchant" key — you do it through an **Enchanting Table** using **XP**. ## Quick steps to enchant a weapon 1. **Craft an Enchanting Table** — 2 Diamond Ore + 4 Cobblestone. 2. **Place it** (right-click with it in hand) and **right-click the table** (or press **E**) to open it. 3. **Select the weapon in your hotbar** — the panel enchants whatever hotbar slot is active (keys **1–9**). 4. **Click an enchantment button** in the panel. Each application costs **3 XP levels** and goes up to **level 3**. ## Getting XP (10 XP points = 1 level) - **Hostile mobs** (zombie, skeleton, spider, creeper): 5 XP each; **boss**: 200 XP - **Passive mobs**: 1–2 XP (babies give nothing) - **Mining ores**: Coal 1 → Diamond 8 XP - **Fishing**: 2 XP per catch XP shows as the green bar above your hotbar, and **it persists through death** — it's the long-term currency. ## Weapon-relevant enchantments | Enchantment | Effect (per level) | Max | Applies to | |---|---|---|---| | **Sharpness** | +2 melee damage | 3 | Weapons | | **Unbreaking** | 20% chance/level to skip durability loss | 3 | Weapons, tools, armor | (There's also **Protection** for armor and **Efficiency** for tools.) Each level costs 3 XP levels, so maxing Sharpness III costs 9 levels (90 XP) and gives **+6 damage**. Enchantments are saved per-item and survive death too. **Example:** earn 3+ levels → place table → right-click → diamond sword in active hotbar slot → click **Sharpness** → +2 damage. Repeat up to 3× for Sharpness III. Full details are in `docs/manual.md` (lines 333–353) and `docs/reference.md` (lines 189–214).
