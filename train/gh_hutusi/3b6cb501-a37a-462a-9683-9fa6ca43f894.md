That note is completely harmless. Here's what it means and why it appears twice: **Why it shows:** Pagefind uses stemming to match search terms across root words (e.g. "running" → "run"). Stemming is a Western-language concept — Chinese doesn't have word inflection, so there's nothing to stem. Pagefind just logs a note that it's skipping that step. **Why twice:** Pagefind likely processes your content in two passes during the build (indexing + language detection), each triggering the same log. **Does it affect search?** No. Chinese full-text search works fine without stemming — Pagefind indexes and matches Chinese characters/tokens correctly. Stemming is only relevant for morphologically-rich languages like English, German, etc. You'll never miss a Chinese result because of this. **Can it be suppressed?** You could potentially pass a `--quiet` flag to Pagefind in the build script, but it would suppress all output including useful info. Not worth it just to hide a note. In short: it's expected, it's just informational, and you can safely ignore it.
