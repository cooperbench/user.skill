Ah, that's a visual/positional metaphor — the dots show where the list continues:

Key design points:
- `..` is our display notation, not jj's `::` — we translate internally
- `x.. N` means "x plus N ancestors" (total = N+1), count excludes x
- `..x N` means "N descendants plus x" (total = N+1), count excludes x
- `..x.. N` means "N each side plus x" (total = 2N+1)
- Bare `x N` auto-appends `..` (ancestors, the common case)
- Bare `x` = just that one commit
