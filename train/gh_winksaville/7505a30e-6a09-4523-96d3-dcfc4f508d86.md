
`cp src/*` only "works" when the glob happens to expand to plain files; the moment it includes a subdirectory, cp skips it with that warning unless you pass `-r`. So `cp` deliberately makes recursion an explicit opt-in. Adopting auto-recursion is actually a *departure* from cp, not parity with it.
