> AGENT

Real signal — and I think dev3 is actually showing the truer picture. The shift is from **measuring "back-to-back rate"** to **measuring "single round-trip latency with parking."**

**Dev2 (INNER=10)**: each sample = average of 10 consecutive round-trips. Inside a sample the main thread never pauses, so the worker thread doesn't have time to park between round-trips — each iteration after the first finds the worker still spinning post-`send()` and into `recv()`. Result: amortized cost per RT looks ~5,600 ns mean and `min` of 274 ns means "10 hot round-trips in a row averaged 274 ns each" (real burst behavior, but not single-RT cost).

**Dev3 (INNER=1)**: every sample = one isolated round-trip with a `now()/record` pause in between. That pause gives the worker enough time to park, so most round-trips pay the futex wake. Mean shifts to ~8,500 ns, which IS the true per-round-trip cost when the worker isn't already hot. The bimodal becomes obvious: `min 190` (rare: still-hot worker), `p1 6,103` (parking already kicks in by p1), `p50 8,631` (typical).
