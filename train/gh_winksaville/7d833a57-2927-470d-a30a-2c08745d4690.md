15	
16	Two-point fit:
17	
18	```
19	framing  = min_low − N_LOW · (min_high − min_low) / (N_HIGH − N_LOW)
20	d(framing)/d(min_low)  = N_HIGH / (N_HIGH − N_LOW) = 1000 / 900 ≈ 1.11
21	d(framing)/d(min_high) = −N_LOW / (N_HIGH − N_LOW) = −100 / 900 ≈ −0.11
22	```

24	Reverse-engineering two observed runs (3900X, `--pin 5,10`, `-D 1`):
25	
26	- Run A: `min_low = 60 ns, min_high = 500 ns` → framing 11.11 ns,
27	  loop/iter 0.4889 ns.

33	numerology; the real defect is ~10 ns of slop on `min_low`.
