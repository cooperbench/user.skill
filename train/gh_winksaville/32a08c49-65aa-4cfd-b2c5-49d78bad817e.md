1	# iiac-perf
2	
3	Measure performance of various Inter-Intra Application Communication
4	(IIAC) techniques.
5	
6	## Design (0.2.0)
7	
8	Design a Rust app that can measure the cost of various (IIAC) techniques.
9	By IIAC I mean all communication techniques between and within
10	applications such as regular function calls vs async function calls vs
11	channels vs serde/deser of json or zero-copy messaging using io_uring and
12	potentially any other technique. It would include between threads or
13	processes or apps on the same computer or between apps on the same LAN or
14	between apps over a WAN.
15	
16	Ideally I'd like to be able to see a histogram of the range of times of the
17	send and receive costs, not just the mean/average of 10^3 or 10^6 invocations.
18	So the cost of the work should or could be something near-zero such as just
19	echoing the input, but maybe there is value in doing some constant but
20	variable amount of work to verify it's in-consequential. One thing I think we
21	need to be aware of is that if we make the work too simple the compiler could
22	just keep things in registers which would not be representative of "real" work.
23	
24	We should start simple, like comparing normal and async function calls and
25	later expand to other techniques.
