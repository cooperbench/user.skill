> AGENT

Third person means the camera is **behind or above your character**, instead of being your character’s eyes. So: **First person** You are the camera. You don’t see your astronaut body, or maybe only hands/tools. - Mouse looks around. - Arrow keys/WASD move you. - Feels immersive, but mouse capture can feel intense in a browser. **Third person** You see the astronaut on the island, and the camera follows them. - Arrow keys move the astronaut. - Camera sits behind them, above them, or orbiting around them. - Often easier to understand where you are because you can see your body relative to the landscape. Examples: - **First person:** Minecraft, Portal, Subnautica, most “walking sim” exploration games. - **Third person:** Zelda, Mario Odyssey, Fortnite, Journey, No Man’s Sky when you switch to external view. For Planetfall, third person could be really nice because the tiny astronaut against the lavender/gold island would sell the stranded-on-a-planet feeling. It’s more work though: we’d need a visible character or at least a simple astronaut marker, camera follow logic, collision/grounding, and probably tuned movement. A softer middle ground is **first person with better escape/prompting**, which is much cheaper and probably enough for now. But longer term, third person might make the island feel more like a place instead of a camera floating over terrain.
