    43→## Use Cases
    44→
    45→- **Library comparison** -- compare downloads, maintenance activity, and dependency count across candidates in one command
    46→- **Maintenance check** -- `last_commit_days`, `commits_30d`, and `issues_closed_30d` reveal whether a project is abandoned, stable, or actively developed
    47→- **Download trends** -- `weekly_downloads` vs `monthly_downloads` (npm, PyPI): `weekly * 4 / monthly > 1` means accelerating adoption
