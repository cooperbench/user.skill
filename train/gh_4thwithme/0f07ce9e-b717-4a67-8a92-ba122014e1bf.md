1	# data-testid Conventions for QA Automation
2	
3	Add `data-testid` attributes to React components (`.jsx` and `.tsx`) when building or modifying user-facing UI that requires QA test coverage.
4	
5	## Naming Convention
6	
7	All `data-testid` values MUST follow this format:
8	
9	```
10	<area>-<element-description>
11	```
12	
13	- **Kebab-case only** — no camelCase, no underscores, no PascalCase
14	- **Area prefix** — indicates the part of the app (e.g., `plp`, `pdp`, `checkout`, `cart`, `header`, `footer`, `nav`, `modal`)
15	- **Element description** — describes the element's purpose, not its implementation
16	
17	### Examples
18	
19	| Element | data-testid |
20	|---------|-------------|
21	| Product card on PLP | `plp-product-card` |
22	| Add to cart button on PDP | `pdp-add-to-cart` |
23	| Search input in header | `header-search-input` |
24	| Quantity selector in cart | `cart-quantity-selector` |
25	| Price display on product card | `plp-product-card-price` |
26	| Filter panel on PLP | `plp-filter-panel` |
27	| Rating stars on PDP | `pdp-rating-stars` |
28	| Checkout submit button | `checkout-submit-button` |
29	
30	### Naming Rules
31	
32	1. Use the most specific area prefix relevant to where the component is rendered
33	2. Keep descriptions concise but unambiguous
34	3. For nested elements, chain descriptors: `plp-product-card-price`, `plp-product-card-image`
35	4. For repeated elements (lists, grids), place the `data-testid` on the individual item, not the container — QA will select by index or content
36	5. For interactive states (modals, dropdowns), prefix with the trigger area: `pdp-size-dropdown`, `pdp-size-dropdown-option`
37

39

55

62	| Modal used from multiple pages | `modal-` | `modal-confirm-delete` |

65	
66	Identify ALL elements a QA engineer would need to target, in this order:
67	
68	1. **Interactive elements** — buttons, links, inputs, selects, checkboxes, radio buttons, toggles
69	2. **Form elements** — form containers, input groups, error messages, validation text
70	3. **Content display elements** — prices, titles, descriptions, images, ratings, counts, badges
