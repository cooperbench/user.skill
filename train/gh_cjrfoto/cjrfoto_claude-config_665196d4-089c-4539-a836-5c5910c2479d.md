## Social Security Credits Explained

### What Are Credits?
The SSA calls them **"credits"** (used to be called "quarters"). You need **40 credits total** to qualify for retirement benefits.

- **Max per year:** 4 credits
- **Years needed:** 10 years of work minimum
- **2024 rate:** You earn 1 credit for every **$1,730 earned** (subject to SE tax)

---

### How You Earn Credits as Self-Employed

You earn credits by **paying Self-Employment (SE) tax** on your net profit.

**SE tax = 15.3%** of your net self-employment income
- 12.4% → Social Security
- 2.9% → Medicare

So if you make **$6,920+ net profit** in a year → you get **all 4 credits** for that year.

---

### The Critical Rule for Expats

This is where most people mess up:

- If you use **Form 2555** (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion) to exclude your income from **income tax**...
- That excluded income **still counts** for SE tax
- So you still pay SE tax → you still earn credits ✅

**You must file Schedule SE even if you owe $0 income tax.**

---

### Totalization Agreements

The US has agreements with some countries to avoid double Social Security taxation. **Colombia is NOT on the list.**

This means:
- You may owe Social Security taxes in **both** the US and Colombia
- Consult a local Colombian accountant about this

---

### Why This Matters

| Age | Benefit |
|-----|---------|
| 62 | Early retirement (reduced) |
| 67 | Full retirement |
| 70 | Maximum benefit |

Your benefit amount depends on your **35 highest-earning years**. The more SE tax you pay now, the higher your future monthly check.

## IRS Streamlined Filing Compliance Procedures

The IRS created a specific program **for Americans abroad who didn't know they had to file.** It's called:

### Streamlined Foreign Offshore Procedures (SFOP)

**What it does:**
- File the last **3 years** of tax returns
- File the last **6 years** of FBAR (if needed)
- **No penalties** — zero, as long as it was non-willful (you didn't know)
- No audit risk if done correctly

**Requirements:**
- Must have lived outside the US for at least 330 days in one of the last 3 years
- Non-willful — you simply didn't know you had to file

---

1. Gather income records for last 3 years (2023, 2024, 2025)
2. File 1040 + Schedule C + Schedule SE for each year
3. File Form 2555 for each year (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion)
4. Pay any SE tax owed (likely small or zero with exclusion)
5. Submit with a **non-willful statement** explaining you didn't know
