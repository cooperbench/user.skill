> AGENT

Here is how refunds work in Razorpay, in simple terms: ## How Razorpay Refunds Work **1. Where the money comes from** When you refund a customer, the money is taken from your **Razorpay account balance** (not directly from your bank). So you need enough balance in Razorpay to issue a refund. If balance is low, Razorpay will auto-debit from your linked bank account. **2. Two types of refunds** | Type | Speed | Cost | |---|---|---| | **Normal Refund** | 5–7 working days | Free | | **Instant Refund** | Within minutes (for UPI/cards that support it) | Small fee (around ₹5–₹10 per refund) | **3. Full or Partial refund** - **Full refund** — return the entire amount the customer paid. - **Partial refund** — return only part of the amount (for example, if only 1 item out of 3 was bad). **4. How to issue a refund** 1. Log in to the Razorpay dashboard. 2. Go to **Transactions → Payments**. 3. Find the customer's payment. 4. Click **"Refund"**. 5. Choose **Full** or **Partial** amount. 6. Choose **Normal** or **Instant**. 7. Confirm. The money goes back to the **same payment method** the customer used (UPI to UPI, card to card, etc.). It cannot be sent to a different account. **5. Time limit** You can refund a payment within **180 days** from the date of the original payment. After that, refunds are not possible through Razorpay — you would have to pay the customer manually. **6. What the customer sees** - For UPI: money returns to their bank account. - For cards: money returns to the card (may show on their statement after a few days). - For wallets (Paytm, etc.): money returns to the wallet. **7. Refund status** You can track every refund in the dashboard under **Transactions → Refunds**. …
