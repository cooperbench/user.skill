Here is how refunds work in Razorpay, in simple terms:

## How Razorpay Refunds Work

**1. Where the money comes from**
When you refund a customer, the money is taken from your **Razorpay account balance** (not directly from your bank). So you need enough balance in Razorpay to issue a refund. If balance is low, Razorpay will auto-debit from your linked bank account.

**2. Two types of refunds**

| Type | Speed | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal Refund** | 5–7 working days | Free |
| **Instant Refund** | Within minutes (for UPI/cards that support it) | Small fee (around ₹5–₹10 per refund) |

**3. Full or Partial refund**
- **Full refund** — return the entire amount the customer paid.
- **Partial refund** — return only part of the amount (for example, if only 1 item out of 3 was bad).

**4. How to issue a refund**
1. Log in to the Razorpay dashboard.
2. Go to **Transactions → Payments**.
3. Find the customer's payment.
4. Click **"Refund"**.
5. Choose **Full** or **Partial** amount.
6. Choose **Normal** or **Instant**.
7. Confirm.

The money goes back to the **same payment method** the customer used (UPI to UPI, card to card, etc.). It cannot be sent to a different account.

**5. Time limit**
You can refund a payment within **180 days** from the date of the original payment. After that, refunds are not possible through Razorpay — you would have to pay the customer manually.

**6. What the customer sees**
- For UPI: money returns to their bank account.
- For cards: money returns to the card (may show on their statement after a few days).
- For wallets (Paytm, etc.): money returns to the wallet.

**7. Refund status**
You can track every refund in the dashboard under **Transactions → Refunds**. Status will show as:
- **Processed** — money sent successfully
- **Pending** — still in progress
- **Failed** — refund could not be completed (rare, usually a bank issue)

**8. Important notes**
- Razorpay's transaction fee (the 2% they charged on the original payment) is **not returned** to you — you bear that small loss.
- You cannot refund more than the original payment amount.
- Refunds cannot be cancelled once initiated.
