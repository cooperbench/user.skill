what is gmv

> AGENT

**GMV = Gross Merchandise Value** — the total don-platform sales value of orders over a period, before subtracting anything (no deductions for refunds, delivery fees, commissions, or costs). It's the headline "how much business flowed through the marketplace" number. In your app it's literally `SUM(orders.total_price)` over a time window — that's exactly what the moderator stats route computes (`gmvThisWeek` in `src/app/api/moderator/stats/route.ts:49`). A few things worth knowing about how it's measured here: - **It's gross.** Your current query sums *all* orders in the window, including `declined`/`cancelled` ones. So GMV ≠ money actually collected or earned. (If you ever want "net," I'd filter those out — I checked earlier and excluding declined/cancelled made no difference to your numbers, but it would once you have declines.) - **It's not revenue.** Revenue would be your commission/margin on that GMV, not the full order value. - **It's zoned** on the moderator dashboard — each moderator sees GMV only for farmers in their region. For your data right now: **this week ₹0** (no orders since 23 May), **all-time ₹4,898** across 43 orders.
