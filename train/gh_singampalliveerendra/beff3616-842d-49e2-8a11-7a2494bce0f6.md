I found the cause. The "Local demand" chart on the farmer dashboard is **not** driven by orders/purchases at all — it's a completely separate data source.

## What feeds the chart

`src/app/farmer/dashboard/page.tsx:194`:

```ts
supabase.from('demand_intents')
  .select('crop_name, quantity_kg')
  .eq('region_slug', farmerData.region_slug)
  .eq('fulfilled', false)
```

So it only counts rows in the **`demand_intents`** table. Those rows are created in exactly one place — the consumer-side **"📣 Raise a Demand Intent"** form (`src/app/consumer/page.tsx:916`), where a buyer manually requests a crop they *want* (crop name, quantity, needed-by date). It's a wishlist/request signal, **not** a record of anything that was actually bought.

## Why it's empty even though farmers sell and consumers buy

Real orders (the `orders` table) never touch this chart. So no matter how many actual purchases happen, "Local demand" stays empty unless one of these is true:

1. **No demand intents exist** for that region — nobody filled the "Raise a Demand Intent" form. (Most likely.)
2. **They're all fulfilled** — the query filters `fulfilled = false`, so intents a moderator marked fulfilled disappear from the chart.
3. **Region mismatch** — the chart matches on `farmer.region_slug`, but the intent's `region_slug` is derived consumer-side from `localStorage` (`yff_consumer_location_name`, lowercased, spaces stripped, default `'tadepalligudem'`) at `consumer/page.tsx:923`. If those strings don't line up exactly, valid intents won't show.

When there's nothing to show it renders the `noDemandSignals` empty state instead of bars (`page.tsx:745-747`) — which is the blank you're seeing.

## What you probably want

It sounds like you expected "Local demand" to reflect **what consumers are actually buying** from farmers in the region. That would mean deriving the bars from the `orders` table (e.g. top produce by quantity/order count in the farmer's region over a recent window), either replacing or supplementing the `demand_intents` source.
