1. What is the Moderator?
The Moderator is a trusted local person — assigned by the GoGrameen founders — who manages the farmer-consumer ecosystem in a specific geographic zone. For the first 3 months, the founders themselves will act as the moderator for the Tadepalligudem zone.

Onboards new farmers in their zone (especially farmers who cannot use smartphones)
Reviews and approves farmer listings before they go live on the consumer page
Monitors supply vs demand and recruits farmers to fill gaps
Handles escalations — delivery delays, quality complaints, payment issues
Manages delivery agents — registers, monitors, activates/deactivates
Sets suggested price ranges per crop so new farmers price sensibly

ℹ  Route: /moderator (or /moderator/dashboard). Login required. Role = "moderator" in the Supabase profiles table. A moderator only sees data for their assigned zone.

Here's the **exact role of the Moderator**, pulled straight from this spec.

## Who the Moderator is

A **trusted local person, hand-picked by the GoGrameen founders**, who runs the entire farmer–consumer ecosystem for **one geographic zone**. Think of them as the **on-ground zone manager / gatekeeper** — not a farmer, not a consumer, not a delivery agent, but the person who supervises all of them in their area.

- First zone: **Tadepalligudem**
- First 3 months: **the founders themselves are the moderator** (until they train a local person to take over)
- They **only see and control their own zone** — never other zones' data

## Their 6 core responsibilities

| # | What they do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Onboard farmers** — including farmers who can't use a smartphone | The moderator registers them on their behalf and creates their `/farmer/{slug}` page |
| 2 | **Approve / reject listings** before they go live | They are the **quality gate** — no farmer produce reaches the consumer page until the moderator approves it |
| 3 | **Monitor supply vs demand** and recruit farmers to fill gaps | If mangoes are scarce, they nudge farmers to grow/list more |
| 4 | **Handle escalations** — delivery delays, quality complaints, payment issues | They are the **complaint resolver** for their zone |
| 5 | **Manage delivery agents** — register, monitor, activate/deactivate | They run the local delivery workforce |
| 6 | **Set suggested price ranges per crop** | So new farmers price sensibly (guidance only, not enforced) |

## The key thing to understand about the role

The moderator is essentially a **human trust-and-quality layer** sitting between farmers and consumers. The platform deliberately does **not** auto-publish farmer listings or let complaints go unmanaged — a real person reviews and controls it all, zone by zone.

Three powers define the role:

1. **Gatekeeper** — nothing reaches buyers without their approval (listings).
2. **Recruiter / balancer** — they actively shape supply to match demand and pull in new farmers + delivery agents.
3. **Dispute resolver** — every complaint in the zone lands on their desk to investigate and close.

## What this means technically (access)

- Logs in at `/moderator` via **phone OTP**
- Must have `role = "moderator"` in the `profiles` table — anyone else is bounced to `/consumer`
- **RLS locks them to their own `region_slug`** — they physically cannot read or edit another zone's farmers/listings
- This area is **completely invisible** to farmers, consumers, and delivery agents

## One distinction worth noting

The spec separates the **moderator** from the **founders**:
- **Moderator** = day-to-day operator of one zone (onboarding, approvals, complaints)
- **Founders** = use the **Reports** feature (Feature 9) to evaluate zone growth from above

For now those are the same people, but the design assumes they'll eventually be different — a moderator running the ground game, founders watching the numbers across zones.

So in one sentence: **the Moderator is the founder-appointed local zone manager who onboards and curates farmers, approves what buyers see, balances supply and demand, manages delivery agents, and resolves all complaints — strictly within their assigned zone.**
