Problems with what I shipped: 1. **`proc.killed` checks the wrong thing** — It's `true` only after `proc.kill()` is called on the JS side, not after `process.kill(-pid)`. So the SIGKILL escalation always fires even if the process already exited, relying on the catch to swallow ESRCH. Works but wrong guard. 2. **Inconsistent settlement paths** — `close`, `error`, `onAbort`, and the timeout handler each call `resolve`/`reject` independently. `cleanup()` only runs from `close`/`error`, not from `onAbort` or timeout. The Promise settling-once semantics paper over what are really unguarded double-resolution paths. 3. **`onAbort` doesn't use `cleanup()`** — It manually clears the timeout but doesn't remove itself (relies on `once`). Inconsistent with the pattern established by `cleanup()`. A cleaner design: single `settle()` function with a `settled` flag that all exit paths go through.
