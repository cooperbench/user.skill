Safe practice is to use `env:` bindings: ```yaml env: INPUT_PHASES: ${{ inputs.phases }} INPUT_AGENT: ${{ inputs.agent }} INPUT_TIMEOUT: ${{ inputs.timeout }} run: | FLAGS="--phases $INPUT_PHASES --agent $INPUT_AGENT --timeout $INPUT_TIMEOUT --no-mcp" ``` ### 3.
