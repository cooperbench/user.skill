> AGENT

Yes — MOMENT-1 is an instance-normalized backbone. **Where the normalization sits:** MOMENT applies RevIN~[Kim et al. 2021] *internally* as the first step of the encoder pipeline, before any patching or attention. Per the MOMENT paper (Goswami et al., 2024) and the `momentfm` reference implementation, every input window is z-scored channel-wise (subtract per-window mean, divide by per-window std) before being broken into 64 patches of 64 timestamps and projected to 512-dim embeddings.
