**`mi_tightness_decomposition.pdf`** — the "Lower bound" bars exceed the `H(E|S) exact` black ticks on Weather and parts of Traffic/Solar at a glance, which looks contradictory for a labeled lower bound (the resolution is in the stacking convention, but it reads wrong).

## Style-level gap vs. top NeurIPS papers

Top-tier figures in this venue typically share:
- Body-matching fonts (CMR/Times) — yours mostly use matplotlib's DejaVu Sans
- Consistent palette across the paper — yours mixes seaborn defaults, custom HTML colors, and matplotlib defaults
- Tight cropping with no whitespace around the frame
- Bold, large axis labels relative to tick labels
