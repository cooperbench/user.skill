229	
230	\textbf{MoE in Foundation Models.} Mixture-of-experts routing~\citep{fedus2022switch} has been used \emph{within} TSFM backbones to scale capacity, with experts placed inside the backbone architecture: Moirai-MoE~\citep{liu2025moiraimoe} routes frequency bands across internal experts, and Time-MoE~\citep{shi2024timemoe} uses sparse activation to reach billions of parameters. 231	
232	\textbf{MoE routing collapse and rescue.} Routing collapse has been framed two ways. \emph{Optimization-side}: load-balancing losses~\citep{fedus2022switch}, z-loss~\citep{zoph2022stmoe}, Expert-Choice routing~\citep{zhou2022expertchoice}, ReLU gating~\citep{zhang2025remoe}, and AdaMix's hidden-state router~\citep{wang2022adamix} all attack training dynamics. \emph{Representation-side}: \citet{chi2022representation} attribute collapse to post-training token clustering around expert centroids and propose hyperspherical routing scores, and \citet{hua2025inputaware} explicitly decouple routing decisions from task optimization to better capture input structure.
