**Verdict: technically accurate but slightly understates the contribution.**

The paper *does* prove a lower bound — Observation 2 part (ii) at `main.tex:1077` lower-bounds the conditional MI loss `I(M,Σ; E | S)`. So "Mutual-Information Lower Bound" is not a stretch. But there are reasons to reconsider:

**Why it works:**
- Honest, specific, mathematically precise
- "Lower Bound" advertises a theorem (reviewers see *proof* before reading)
- Matches the paper's actual artifact (Observation 2 part ii)

**Why it slightly undersells:**
1. The paper's headline theoretical artifact is a **decomposition + bound + signal-ratio predictor** — three things, not just a bound. The abstract itself says "*mutual-information decomposition that yields a signal-ratio predictor*" — not "lower bound."
2. "Lower Bound" alone is ambiguous out of context: a lower bound on *what*? Reviewers will pause. (It's a lower bound on routing information *lost*, which is a negative quantity — semantically inverted from the usual "we prove a lower bound on accuracy" pattern.)
3. The bound is part (ii) of a multi-part theorem; isolating it in the title spotlights the narrowest piece.

**Stronger second-clause candidates** (same prefix, sharper second clause):

1. *Diagnosing Routing Collapse in Time Series Foundation Models: **A Mutual-Information Decomposition and Its Causal Fix***
   — matches the paper's own framing in the abstract; "Decomposition" is the term you actually use

2. *Diagnosing Routing Collapse in Time Series Foundation Models: **A Mutual-Information Bound, a Signal-Ratio Predictor, and a Causal Fix***
   — names all three theoretical artifacts; longer but maximally informative

3. *Diagnosing Routing Collapse in Time Series Foundation Models: **A Mutual-Information Account and Its Causal Fix***
   — "Account" subsumes decomposition + bound + predictor; broadest

4. *Diagnosing Routing Collapse in Time Series Foundation Models: **An Information-Theoretic Bound and Its Causal Fix***
   — drops "Lower" (avoids the ambiguity); reads cleaner
