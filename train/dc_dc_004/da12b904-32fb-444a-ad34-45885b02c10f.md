## What We Deliberately Don't Extract

- **No shared `useAuthSession`**: Layout's auth guard and HomePage's auth flow are fundamentally different concerns (simple tri-state guard vs OAuth/PWA/referral linking). Merging would over-abstract.
- **No switch to shared `useFileDragTracking`**: CharacterAnimate's drag handlers have scroll-blocking + MIME pre-validation that the shared hook doesn't support.
- **No extraction of settings sync effects** in CharacterAnimatePage: 4 small effects tightly coupled to 6+ local state setters; extracting creates worse API than inline.
- **No extraction of Layout pane styling math**: Pure CSS derivations from pane state - just computation, not behavior.
