Clean build. Here's what was happening and the fix:

**Problem**: On touch devices, `onTouchEnd` fires even after drag/scroll gestures. All three rendering modes in `VideoItem` (collage, mobile poster, HoverScrubVideo) were calling `onMobileTap`/`onLightboxOpen` on every `touchend` regardless of whether the user was tapping or dragging/scrolling.

**Fix**: Added a `touchStartRef` to track the initial touch position. Each `onTouchStart` records `{x, y}`, and each `onTouchEnd` checks if the finger moved more than 10px — if so, it's a drag/scroll and the handler returns early without opening the lightbox. Applied to all three touch paths in `VideoItem.tsx`.
