# Plan: Structural Decomposition & Props Reduction

## Context

The decruftify strict score is 91/100, with 222 wontfixed findings (mostly structural T3/T4 and props). After thorough evaluation of every finding, this plan identifies the decompositions that actually improve the codebase vs. those that are fine as-is. The goal: reduce the largest god components and bloated interfaces to manageable sizes by extracting self-contained pieces.

**Guiding principle**: Extract when a component mixes distinct responsibilities. Skip when size is justified by cohesion (a 500-LOC drag-drop handler that does one complex thing well is fine).

**Fix**: Instead of props drilling, have parent (`ImageLightbox` / `VideoLightbox`) render the sub-panels directly based on mode, with ControlsPanel becoming a thin layout wrapper that receives children.

**Target**: 92 props → ~5 props (variant, children, currentMediaId)
**Modify**: `src/shared/components/MediaLightbox/components/ControlsPanel.tsx`, parent lightbox files
