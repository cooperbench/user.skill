**Beautiful? No. Elegant? In places.** But it's something more interesting than either.

This codebase has the character of **a product that survived contact with reality and is being systematically cleaned up**. The trajectory is more impressive than the current snapshot.

**What's genuinely good:**
- **Naming** is excellent throughout. `getGenerationId()`, `resolveProjectResolution()`, `useShotsQueries()` — you read the name, you know what it does.
- **The params-driven routing** in `complete_task` is elegant. Intent declared at creation time, no type-switching at completion. That's a real design insight.
- **queryKeys.ts** as a single registry, **errorHandler.ts** as centralized error categorization, **settingsResolution.ts** for priority chains — these are the kind of small, right abstractions that make a codebase livable.

- There are still 800-1100 line components (ShotEditor at 1,130 lines, PhaseConfigVertical at 775). They work, but they're not beautiful.
- ShotEditor takes ~90 props. That's not elegance, that's a component doing too much.

- The context migration (replacing prop drilling) is in flight but incomplete.

**The character I'd give it:** pragmatic, improving, self-aware. The TODOs know exactly what's wrong. The CLAUDE.md knows what good looks like. The recent commits ("desloppify," test campaign, decomposition wave) show someone who cares about the craft but ships first and cleans second.

It's not the kind of codebase you'd put in a textbook. It's the kind of codebase that actually works, that's been through real users and real pressure, and is being made better with discipline rather than rewrites. That's worth more than elegance.
