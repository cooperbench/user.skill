Pushed as `4b33ff5`. **Short answer:** expected behaviour, wasteful fix applied. Each `-p` you publish in the inner Docker daemon spawns one `docker-proxy` binary per address family (IPv4 + IPv6), each ~5 MB RSS. Your nested Sandcastle is publishing ports 3027–3099 (plus 8080/8443/3050…), which is why you see ~150 processes and close to a gig of resident memory for port forwarding alone. Fix: added `--userland-proxy=false` to both `entrypoint.sh` and `docker-restart.sh` so the inner `dockerd` uses hairpin NAT via iptables instead of spawning proxy processes. After the next sandbox image rebuild, rebuilding or recreating your sandboxes will eliminate them entirely. No functional change — container-to-host and host-to-container port forwarding still work, they just go through the kernel's nat table. Want me to release a patch?
