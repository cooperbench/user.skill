#### `apps/web/src/components/file-browser/ShareDialog.tsx` (line 149)

**Severity: CRITICAL | Zeroed `folderKeyBytes` passed to recursive `collectChildKeys`**

At line 137, `folderKeyBytes.fill(0)` zeroes the subfolder key. Then at lines 139-141, the **zeroed** `folderKeyBytes` is passed as the `folderKey` parameter to the recursive `collectChildKeys` call.
