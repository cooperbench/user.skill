### 2. No `--testnet` or `--network` Flag in the Phala CLI

The `phala` CLI (v1.1.13) has **no `--testnet` or `--network` flag**. All CVM deployments go to Phala Cloud's production infrastructure. The deploy command supports `--region`, `--instance-type`, `--kms` (phala/ethereum/base), but there is no concept of a separate testnet deployment target.

### 3. No Dedicated Testnet or Sandbox Environment

Phala Cloud does **not** offer a separate testnet or sandbox environment for CVM deployment. There is no "devnet" or "testnet" tier. The free credits and free CVM **are** effectively your sandbox -- you deploy to the same production infrastructure but consume your free allocation.
