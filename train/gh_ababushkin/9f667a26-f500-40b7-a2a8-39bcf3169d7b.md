## Root cause

`/stock-signal` sets `model_ready=NO` when PEG/PS falls outside the PASS band, and `/stock-model` refuses to run when `model_ready≠YES`. ## Fix direction

Covered tickers bypass the gate. `/stock-model` runs unconditionally for any ticker in `COVERAGE.md` and surfaces the signal verdict (PASS / WATCH / CAUTION / FAIL) in the model output rather than refusing. Off-coverage tickers keep the current gate behaviour.

Open design question to resolve before implementation: **bypass** the gate vs **recalibrate** the GARP thresholds per profile (ESTABLISHED INCUMBENT mega-caps need a different PEG band than EMERGING SaaS). Bypass is simpler and ships today; recalibration is the right long-term fix. Likely answer: bypass now for covered tickers (unblocks the workflow), recalibrate later as a separate issue.
