Situation (The Starting Point) The introduction begins by establishing a shared context or "opening scene" that is non-controversial. Complication (The Disturbing Event) The complication is the "disturbing event" that creates tension by changing the stable situation. It reveals an Undesired Result (R1)—a problem that must be solved or a threat that must be addressed. Technical focus: This could be an external shift (e.g., a new technology or competitor), an internal change (e.g., a system not working as intended), or a recently recognized performance lag. 3. Question The complication naturally triggers a question in the reader's mind. This is the pivot upon which the entire document depends. Technical focus: The answer provides the solution that closes the gap between the problematic R1 and the successful R2.
