Why the fuck do we have "_app" in the filename for for route: "/"?

> AGENT

Because `_app` is a **pathless layout route**, not a URL segment. In this repo: - [`web/src/routes/_app.tsx`](/Users/tanishqpalandurkar/Projects/better-ao/web/src/routes/_app.tsx:5) mounts `WorkspaceLayout`. - [`web/src/routes/_app.index.tsx`](/Users/tanishqpalandurkar/Projects/better-ao/web/src/routes/_app.index.tsx:5) is that layout’s index child, so it renders at `/`. - [`web/src/routes/_app.terminal.$sessionId.tsx`](/Users/tanishqpalandurkar/Projects/better-ao/web/src/routes/_app.terminal.$sessionId.tsx:5) is another child, rendered at `/terminal/$sessionId`. The underscore tells TanStack Router: “use this as a grouping/layout segment, but don’t put `app` in the URL.” So `_app.index.tsx` maps to `/`, not `/_app` or `/app`. The reason it exists is to let `/` Kanban and `/terminal/$sessionId` share the same app shell: sidebar, topbar, workspace query/context, preferences, and selection logic. The name `_app` is just a convention for “app shell”.
