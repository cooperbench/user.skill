> DEVELOPER

can I use 2 ChatGPT Pro subs at a time>

> AGENT

Short answer: **not on one account in the normal way**.

- OpenAI says if you subscribe on multiple platforms (iOS/Android/web) without canceling the old one first, you can end up with **multiple active subscriptions and multiple charges**.[OpenAI Help Center: avoid being charged twice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001043)  
- Subscriptions are **not transferable between accounts**; to use a different account you must cancel first and buy a new one.[OpenAI Help Center: transfer to new account](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9135236-how-to-transfer-a-chatgpt-plus-or-chatgpt-pro-subscription-to-a-new-account)  
- You can be signed into two ChatGPT accounts in one session, but their billing/subscriptions are separate and not merged.[OpenAI Help Center: account switching](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001068-use-multiple-accounts-with-account-switching)

So practically: you can use one Pro plan per account, and if you want two Pro setups, they must be on separate accounts (with separate subscriptions).

Yes—this is supported as long as you use **two separate ChatGPT accounts**.

You can:
1. Stay signed in to two accounts in the same session.
2. Switch instantly via account switching (no log out needed).
3. Use them as separate plans/payments; subscriptions do not merge or combine across accounts.

So for your use case, if one Pro account hits limits, you can switch to the other account and continue (within that account’s own limits). Just note:
- Quotas/restrictions are enforced per account and can still include temporary usage limits.
- OpenAI says subscriptions are not transferable between accounts (no merge/transfer).
