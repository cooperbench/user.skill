Operators cannot tell the guard is inert. 2. **Narrow `PATH` in the handler process.** `ShellHookHandler` in `crates/plugins/src/shell_hook.rs:101-117` spawns `sh -c <command>` inheriting the gateway process environment. When Moltis runs as a systemd service, even with `ExecStart=/bin/bash -lc 'moltis …'`, the inherited `PATH` often excludes `$HOME/.local/bin`, so `command -v dcg` fails even though `dcg` is installed for the service user. The fix is the same either way.
