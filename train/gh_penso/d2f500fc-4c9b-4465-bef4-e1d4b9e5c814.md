It does NOT re-run model discovery (calling `/v1/models`) first. The flow: 1. **At startup**: `ProviderRegistry::fire_discoveries()` calls `/v1/models` for each provider, discovered models get registered via `register_openai_compatible_providers()` — this is the only time `/v1/models` is called. 2. **When "Detect All Models" is clicked**: `detect_supported()` at `crates/chat/src/lib.rs:1939-1957` reads `reg.list_models()` and probes each one. No new models are discovered — it just checks whether the already-registered models respond to a ping.
