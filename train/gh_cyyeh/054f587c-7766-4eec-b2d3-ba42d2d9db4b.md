> AGENT

This means your Vite dev server is trying to proxy the `/api/health` request to your backend server, but the backend isn't running or isn't reachable.

The `AggregateError` with `internalConnectMultiple` indicates the proxy tried multiple connection attempts (likely to `localhost` on whatever port your backend is configured on) and all failed.

**Fix:** Start your backend server. The frontend's Vite proxy is configured to forward `/api/*` requests to it, and it can't connect.
