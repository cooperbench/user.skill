Two bug fixes in `pkg/game/session.go`, both reported by QA:

**Defect 1 — Bag not consumed after first spawn (`pieceSpawned`, line ~202)**

**Defect 2 — First hold didn't set `HoldUsed`; double-hold allowed in same turn (`PlayerHolds`, line ~169)**

Fix: added `s.HoldUsed = true` after `pieceSpawned()` returns in the `KindNone` branch, overriding the reset.

Also updated `TestPlayerHoldsSwapRestoresPiece` (which was written against the buggy behavior) and split it into two tests: one verifying the second hold is blocked in the same turn, one verifying the swap is allowed after a new piece spawns.
