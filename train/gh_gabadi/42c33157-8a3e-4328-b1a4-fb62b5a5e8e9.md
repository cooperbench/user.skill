12	**Root cause of a stuck queue (protocol-level bug):** `executed` is NEVER written to the RECEIVER's logbook — only to the sender's. `deliver.sh` writes `executing` to receiver logbook. `notify-agent.sh` writes `sent`+`executed` to the SENDER's logbook. Therefore after ANY delivery, the receiver's logbook permanently shows `executing` as last terminal state. The stop-hook (`stop-hook-<role>.sh`) that would deliver pending messages also checks for `executed` in the receiver logbook — which also never appears. The whole pending-delivery mechanism is broken by design. In practice the agent's OWN `sent`+`executed` entries (from its own handoff calls) satisfy the check IF they exist — but if they were overwritten by a concurrent write or the agent's session ran from a different CWD, they may be absent.
13

If the agent finishes WITHOUT sending a downstream handoff, or its handoff process dies before the logbook append, the `executing` entry is orphaned. One missing terminal write freezes the receiver's pending queue forever, silently.
15
