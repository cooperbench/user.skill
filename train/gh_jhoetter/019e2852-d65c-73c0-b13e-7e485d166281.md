Sustainability vs Energy:
- **Energy Lens / Energieberatung** is operational energy and German regulatory handoff:
  - thermal envelope
  - U-values
  - heated/unheated zones
  - GEG / iSFP / BEG preparation
  - export to tools like Hottgenroth, BKI, ETU, ZUB Helena
  - focus: building energy performance and renovation consulting

- **Sustainability Lens / Nachhaltigkeit, Ökobilanz** is embodied impact and material lifecycle:
  - embodied carbon
  - EPDs
  - recycled content
  - reuse/circularity
  - lifecycle assessment
  - material passports
  - focus: what the building is made of and its environmental footprint

They overlap on materials, but they are not the same. Example: mineral wool thermal conductivity belongs to Energy; EPD/GWP and recyclability belong to Sustainability.

Facility Operations:
Yes, it **can** be a lens, but it is a different kind of lens.

It is not a design/planning lens like Architecture or Structure. It is a **lifecycle/use-phase lens**:
- asset register
- maintenance intervals
- warranty data
- serial numbers
- equipment access
- room/system ownership
- handover data for operators

Whether it should be in the first lens set depends on product strategy. I’d classify it as:

- **Core early lenses:** Architecture, Structure, MEP, Coordination, Energy
- **Strong next lenses:** Fire Safety, Cost/Quantity, Sustainability
- **Lifecycle/enterprise lens:** Facility Operations

So your surprise is reasonable. Facility Operations is valid as a lens, but probably not a near-term default unless the product explicitly targets owners/operators after handover.
