should the materials also have some kind of surface change, or just color? e.g. a brick wall would be different in texture etc, right? how does revit handle this? ...

> AGENT

Yes. A material should be more than color if we want Revit-like behavior.

Revit separates material behavior roughly like this:

- **Graphics**: what you see in non-rendered architectural views: shading color, transparency, surface pattern, cut pattern.
- **Appearance**: rendered/realistic look: image textures, tint/color, reflectivity/roughness, transparency, bump/relief maps, scale, rotation, offset.
- **Physical/Thermal**: engineering properties, when present.

So a brick wall in Revit is not just “red/brown.” It can have:
- a **surface pattern** in plan/elevation/hidden-line style views,
- an **appearance texture image** in realistic/rendered views,
- a **bump/relief map** that makes mortar/brick edges catch light,
- texture **scale/rotation/position** controls,
- sometimes physical/thermal material data.

Important distinction: Revit typically does **not** model every brick as geometry. The surface looks like brick through patterns, image maps, and bump/relief shading. Actual modeled brick courses would be separate geometry/detailing, not the base material itself.

For our app, the right direction is:

1. Keep `baseColor`, `roughness`, `metalness`, transparency as the fallback.
2. Add material appearance maps: `albedoMap`, `normalMap` or `bumpMap`, maybe `roughnessMap`.
3. Add texture transform controls: scale, rotation, offset.
4. Add 2D graphics patterns for architectural views: surface pattern and cut pattern.
5. Use procedural fallbacks where we do not have bitmap assets yet, e.g. brick grid, concrete noise, wood grain, standing seam, tile.

Right now the app is mostly at step 1, with a few special procedural cases like cladding boards. To feel like Revit, brick/concrete/wood/tile need visible surface treatment, not just color.

Sources: Autodesk docs on Revit material assets and appearance/texture controls: [Material Properties and Assets](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2023/ENU/Revit-Customize/files/GUID-8D1A49AB-849C-49DF-A7B9-34C596E0C6F2.htm), [Change Material Appearance](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2025/ENU/Revit-Customize/files/GUID-03A2730E-AAEE-42F6-88BC-3C9718B9F5DD.htm), [Texture Editor](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/ENU/Revit-Customize/files/GUID-33770911-426A-4E53-B91D-67AB64110E19.htm), [Relief/Bump](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2024/ENU/RevitLT-Customize/files/GUID-71574CD7-4031-4E67-B1EF-A5A2264230AE.htm).
