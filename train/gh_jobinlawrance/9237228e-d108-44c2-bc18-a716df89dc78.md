## The problem: the formats are incompatible

Your Ollama models are **GGUF** (llama.cpp format). oMLX runs **MLX** format (Apple's framework — safetensors converted for Metal). They are not interchangeable file formats — you can't move the blobs in `~/.ollama/models/blobs/` into oMLX and have them load. oMLX's own workflow is "search and download MLX models from HuggingFace in the admin dashboard," i.e. it expects MLX-format weights only.

So "move the models without redownloading" isn't physically possible — the GGUF weights aren't valid MLX inputs. There's no clean file-move or in-place reuse.

What you actually have (12 GB total in Ollama):

| Ollama model | Type | MLX equivalent (HF `mlx-community`) |
|---|---|---|
| `llama3.1:8b` (4.6G) | LLM | `Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-4bit` |
| `deepseek-r1:8b` (4.6G) | LLM | `DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B-4bit` |

| `mxbai-embed-large` (639M) | embedding | `mxbai-embed-large-v1-mlx` |

These are *different downloads* (re-quantized for MLX), but each is smaller than its GGUF counterpart, and oMLX pulls them in one click from the admin dashboard.
