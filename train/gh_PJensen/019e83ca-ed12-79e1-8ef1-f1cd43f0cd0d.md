Penalty options, from least to most punishing: - **Light:** Keep gear, lose gold or score, return portal available. - **Medium:** Keep equipped gear, drop backpack at death tile, return portal available. - **Hard-softcore:** Drop everything except one equipped weapon/armor slot, return portal available. - **Checkpoint:** Reload last bed save, place return portal to death location. This is simpler conceptually but can feel weird because world progress rolls back. I’d choose **medium**: keep equipped gear, drop carried inventory/gold at the death tile, resurrect in town with a return portal. That preserves roguelike danger without making normal mode delete the run. It also creates a strong recovery story: “go reclaim your body.” You’re not doing something stupid. Pure permadeath is genre-correct, but it narrows the audience and makes experimentation expensive.
