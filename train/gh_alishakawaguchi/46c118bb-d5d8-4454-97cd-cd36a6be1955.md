Yes, `IsPaneDead` is valuable. It's the **only** early-exit mechanism in `WaitFor` when the agent process crashes or exits unexpectedly. Here's the flow: `WaitFor` polls in a loop waiting for a regex pattern to appear in the tmux pane output. Without `IsPaneDead` (line 124), if the Droid process crashes mid-test, `WaitFor` would keep polling the (now static) pane content for the **full timeout** (often 30s–2min) before failing. With it, the loop detects the process is gone and returns immediately with a clear "process exited" error. The `remain-on-exit` tmux option is key — it keeps the pane visible after the process exits (so you can still capture its output for diagnostics), but marks `#{pane_dead}` as `1`. Without `remain-on-exit`, tmux would destroy the pane entirely and `Capture()` would fail. So it's not just valuable — it's essential for fast failure in E2E tests. Removing it would make every agent-crash scenario wait the full timeout before reporting.
