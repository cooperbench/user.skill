## Approach Since the backend already supports `page`/`per_page` and we're doing client-side search, there's really one clean approach: **Fetch all projects from the API** (set `per_page=100` which is the backend max), then do client-side filtering by search query and client-side pagination (12 per page). Pagination shows "Previous / Next" or page numbers at the bottom.
