cool, then should we remove the version filter in the subscription or we keep it, what's your opinion?

> AGENT

I'd keep it. They serve different purposes: - **Source-level version filters** — control what gets ingested/shown globally for that source. Applies to all subscribers. - **Subscription-level version filter** — lets individual notification channels filter further. One team might only care about major releases, another about everything. Example: A source includes all non-prerelease versions, but a Slack channel subscription filters to `^\d+\.0\.0$` to only get notified on majors. Removing the subscription filter would force everyone subscribed to a source to get the same notifications, which loses flexibility. The cost of keeping it is near zero.
