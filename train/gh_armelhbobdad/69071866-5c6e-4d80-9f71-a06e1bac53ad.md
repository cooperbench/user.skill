### What I did, and the call I made on the fix shape

Issue **#299** had hit the same bug in `skf-export-skill` back in May and was resolved with a **consumer-side glob + JSON-envelope fallback** in `load-skill.md §4b`. I mirrored that prior art instead:

1. Glob `{forge_version}/test-report-{skill_name}-*.md`, sort by parsed ISO timestamp, take newest.
2. Fall back to the flat-path glob.

That's strictly better than the suggested producer-side fix for three reasons: consistency with the existing consumer pattern, no new artifact to maintain, and it works **retroactively** for every test report already on disk (the producer-side fix would only have helped future runs).
