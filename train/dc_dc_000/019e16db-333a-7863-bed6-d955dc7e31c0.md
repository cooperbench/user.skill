3. **What Both Prior Reviewers Missed**

The plan under-specifies the **template compatibility boundary**. Sprint 3 says leaf-template/plan-template replaces executor/orchestrator conceptually while “pack layout stays,” then Sprint 4b removes legacy `OrchestratorDefinition` YAML. Third: the **remote-artifact adapter is too big for Sprint 3**. Dispatch, polling, artifact fetch, checksum, retry state, and partial failure semantics are basically a small job system. Treating it as equal-sized with `local` and `manual` hides complexity.
