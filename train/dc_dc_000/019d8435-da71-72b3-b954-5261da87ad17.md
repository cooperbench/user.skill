## Overview

`useClipEditing.ts` (597 lines) in `reigh-app/src/tools/video-editor/hooks/` is a monolithic hook with 22 `useCallback`s that handles deletion, splitting, audio, positioning, text overlays, and generic clip updates. The goal is to extract domain-specific callbacks into 5 focused hooks under `hooks/clip-editing/`, keeping a barrel `useClipEditing.ts` that composes them for backward compatibility.

**Key constraint:** Many callbacks share internal infrastructure (`applyRowsEdit`, `applyConfigEdit`, `isPinnedGroupMember`, `notifyPinnedGroupEditBlocked`, `getValidClipIds`, `currentTimeRef`). These must be extracted into a shared context object that the parent hook creates and passes to each sub-hook.

**Proposed groupings:**

| Sub-hook | Callbacks | Approx lines |
|---|---|---|
| `useClipCoreEditing` (shared infra) | `applyRowsEdit`, `applyConfigEdit`, `isPinnedGroupMember`, `notifyPinnedGroupEditBlocked`, `getValidClipIds`, `currentTimeRef`, `onOverlayChange`, `handleUpdateClips`, `handleUpdateClipsDeep`, `handleSelectedClipChange` | ~180 |
| `useClipDeletion` | `handleDeleteClips`, `handleDeleteClip` | ~30 |
