        # Implementation Plan: Unify Timeline and Gallery Selection

## Overview

**Problem:** Timeline selection (`useMultiSelect` → `TimelineEditorContext`) and gallery selection (`GallerySelectionContext`) are fully independent. Users can have items selected in both simultaneously. A plain click in one area doesn't clear the other.

**Goal:** Make them act as one unified selection pool:
- Plain click in either → clears BOTH, selects just that item
- Cmd+click → toggles individual items across both sources (preserves the other's selection)
- Escape → clears everything
- AgentChat continues merging both sources for attachments (already works, no change needed)

**Architecture context:**
- `GallerySelectionContext` is an app-level provider (`AppProviders.tsx:47`)
- `TimelineEditorContext` is nested inside the video editor tool
- `useTimelineState.ts` already wraps `useMultiSelect` methods and provides them to the context
- `GenerationsPaneGallery.tsx` handles gallery click/lasso selection
- `VideoEditorShell.tsx` wires keyboard shortcuts

**Approach:** Two-direction coordination without a new context:
1. **Timeline → Gallery:** `useTimelineState` imports `useGallerySelection` and calls `clearGallerySelection()` in its wrapped `selectClip` (non-toggle) and `selectClips` methods.
2. **Gallery → Timeline:** `GallerySelectionContext` gains a `registerPeerClear` mechanism. The video editor registers timeline's `clearSelection` as the peer clear. Gallery calls it on non-toggle/non-append selects.
3. **Escape:** `VideoEditorShell.tsx` passes a combined clear to `useKeyboardShortcuts` that clears both.

This avoids new contexts, module singletons, or effect-based coordination. Both clear functions are idempotent so there's no recursion risk.
