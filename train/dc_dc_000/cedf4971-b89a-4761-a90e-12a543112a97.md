### 8. **Why Stale Items Might Not Appear When Queue Is Empty**

**Most likely scenario:**
1. ✅ Stale dimension detected and injected into `plan.queue_order`
2. ✅ Plan synced during scan (`scan_workflow.py` lines 493-494)
3. ❌ **Stale dimension's score >= threshold (default 95.0)**
   - Example: Stale dimension has score 98.0%, threshold 95.0 → filtered at line 294 in helpers.py
4. ❌ No other queue items exist
5. Result: Empty queue, "Nothing to do!" message

**Other scenarios:**
- **Fresh scan after review**: Stale dimension refreshed → no longer stale → pruned from plan
- **High objective backlog**: Stale injection blocked by 3-scan cadence gate
- **No dimension_scores**: Entire subjective system offline
- **Cadence gate**: Last injection too recent, cadence blocks re-injection
