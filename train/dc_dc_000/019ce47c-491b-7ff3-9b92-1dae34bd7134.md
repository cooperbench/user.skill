Restructure mechanical backlog presentation in triage                                                 
                                                                                                     
 Context                                                                                               

 With backlog-first queue semantics, triage's reflect stage is the gatekeeper for all execution work.
 It now builds a unified execution plan from two sources:

 1. Review/concerns findings — LLM design issues verified by observe, clustered by reflect
 2. Mechanical backlog — detector-created objective issues, backlog by default

 Currently the mechanical backlog is a flat sample of 20 alphabetically-sorted individual issues at
 the bottom of the prompt. Three problems:

 - Auto-clusters are invisible: auto/ clusters that already group mechanical issues (with actions,
 autofix commands) aren't passed to triage at all — collect_triage_input only passes epic/-prefixed
 clusters.
 - Unclustered items are undifferentiated: Issues from needs_judgment detectors (28 detectors —
 smells, orphaned, uncalled_functions, etc.) and singletons are mixed with auto-clustered items in one
  flat list.
 - No discovery path: The backlog section doesn't tell the agent how to dig deeper (desloppify
 backlog, desloppify show <id>), even though those commands are listed in the system prompt.

 The reflect stage needs to see the mechanical backlog organized the way it actually exists —
 auto-clusters as promotable units, unclustered items as individual decisions — alongside the review
 findings it already handles, so it can build one coherent prioritized execution plan.

 How review findings and mechanical backlog relate

 After triage, both types of work land in the same queue_order:
 - epic/ clusters from review findings (reflect creates these)
 - auto/ clusters promoted from mechanical backlog (reflect selects these)
 - Individual mechanical issues promoted ad-hoc

 Reflect should reason about interactions: e.g., "auto/unused-imports overlaps with the files touched
 by epic/api-cleanup — promote it and do them together" or "auto/test-coverage is 40 issues but the
 review findings already have a test cluster — leave the auto-cluster, it's redundant." The unified
 view makes this possible.

- Persisted issue kinds:
  - `mechanical_finding`
  - `review_finding`
  - `concern_finding`

- Separate persisted assessment store:

  - `workflow_action`
  - `subjective_dimension`

- scan merge updates findings and review requests

- backlog only sees `mechanical_finding`
