The goal is to first try resolving the overlap by snapping the dropped clip to the nearest edge of the overlapping clip — keeping it on the same track — and only fall back to track switching if snapping can't work. **Core change:** A new utility function `trySnapToEdge` in `coordinate-utils.ts`, called before `findNearestFreeTrack` at all 5 call sites.
