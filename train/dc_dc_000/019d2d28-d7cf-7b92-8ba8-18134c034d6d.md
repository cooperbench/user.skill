It's NOT a literal while loop — it's an agentic process where the loop state machine drives the agent through phases, and sub-agents do the deep work. The main thread stays high-level: reading summaries, updating the plan, dispatching workers. ## Key design requirements 1. Two phases: plan and execute, cycling. The first plan phase captures the loop spec (goal, command, success criteria, allowed changes, optimization strategy, bug-finding approach, philosophy). Subsequent plan phases UPDATE the spec based on what the last execution found.
