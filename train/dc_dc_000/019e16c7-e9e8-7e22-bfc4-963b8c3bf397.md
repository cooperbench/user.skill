The bridge-candidate story is internally inconsistent. §3 says `bridge_candidates` get single `topic_id` and `proposed_by_bot_id` (§3, line 82), but §17.M says add `dyad_id` and “decide later whether to add `topic_id`/`bot_id`” (§17, line 479).
