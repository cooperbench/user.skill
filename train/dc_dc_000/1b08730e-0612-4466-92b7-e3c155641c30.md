130	    Subdirectories and dot-prefixed names are skipped. Malformed files are

162	    """Move ``file_path`` to ``dest_dir/<sha256>``.
163	
164	    The sha256-of-file-bytes filename (FLAG-P8-002) avoids any post-hoc
