### Step 8: Three new reviewers (`tools/reviewers/visual_quality.py`, `tools/reviewers/speaker_flow.py`, `tools/reviewers/overlay_fit.py` — new)
**Scope:** Medium

1. **`visual_quality.py`**: for each clip, compute the visual trim range and find intersections with the asset's `video_dead` zones (from Step 4's cache). Emit `flag` `visual_dead_overlap` when overlap ≥ `max(0.5s, 15% of clip duration)`. `auto_fix` path: none (no writable visual-trim field; consistent with FLAG-001/correctness-1). Scope limited to dead-zone overlap per the idea brief's minimal framing (answers `scope-1` — no new policy layer).
2. **`speaker_flow.py`**: iterate arrangement dialogue clips in order; for each consecutive pair on the audio track look up each clip's `pool_entry.speaker` via the pool (passed through `EnrichedArrangement`). Emit `flag` `speaker_repeat_without_break` when two adjacent dialogue clips have the *same non-None speaker* with no intervening b-roll stinger AND non-contiguous `trim_sub_range`. Emit nothing when either speaker is `None` (this is the `correctness-2` fix: ground on `pool.speaker`, not pool-id prefix).
3. **`overlay_fit.py`**: **flag-only, no auto_fix.** For each `overlay`-role clip, emit `flag`s:
   - `overlay_same_source_as_audio` when `visual_source.pool_id == audio_source.pool_id`.
   - `overlay_insufficient_coverage` when the overlay clip duration `< _MIN_OVERLAY_COVERAGE_SEC` (imported from `tools/cut.py`) OR when hold ratio would exceed `_MAX_VISUAL_HOLD_RATIO` (also imported). Reusing the existing constants answers `scope-1` and `FLAG-001`/`correctness-1`/`callers-2`: no second, conflicting policy layer and no schema growth.
