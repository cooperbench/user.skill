## Overview

`useTimelineData.ts` (678 LOC) is the last god hook in the video editor. It is called in exactly one place — `useTimelineState.ts:25` — which already follows the "thin orchestrator" pattern for downstream hooks (useClipEditing, useAssetManagement, etc.). The return type `UseTimelineDataResult` is imported by 6 other hooks for typing, and `ClipTab`/`SaveStatus`/`RenderStatus` types are re-exported by `useTimelineState.ts`.

**Dependency graph of the 6 concerns:**
- **UI Preferences** — independent (only needs `timelineId` for storage key)
- **Data Fetching** — independent (only needs `provider`, `timelineId`)
- **Derived Data** — depends on `data` state
- **Render State** — depends on `resolvedConfig` and `renderMetadata` (from Derived)
- **Asset Operations** — depends on `provider`, `queryClient`, `timelineId`, `userId`, `commitData` (from Save/Sync)
- **Save/Sync** — the core engine; depends on Data Fetching (`saveMutation`), and owns `data` state, `commitData`, edit operations, and poll sync effects

**Key constraint:** `commitData` is the central function connecting save/sync to everything else. It manages `data` state, selection state, and triggers saves. The orchestrator will get `commitData` from the save hook and pass it to asset operations.
