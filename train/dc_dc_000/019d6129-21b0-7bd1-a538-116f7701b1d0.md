        Idea:
Fix video and audio clip resize behavior in the video editor. Currently when you stretch or shorten a video clip by dragging its edge, the system adjusts the trim points (from/to) without changing speed, which causes the video to freeze on the last frame or cut off when extended beyond source duration. The fix: (1) VIDEO CLIPS - when resizing, adjust speed instead of trim points so the full video always plays. Stretching a clip = slower playback, shortening = faster. The formula: newSpeed = (originalTo - originalFrom) / newTimelineDuration. The from/to (source trim) should stay fixed during right-edge resize. Left-edge resize should still adjust from (trim start) but also recalculate speed to maintain the end point. (2) AUDIO CLIPS - never adjust speed, never let the clip extend beyond the actual audio duration. Clamp the resize so the user cannot drag the right edge past (assetEntry.duration - from) / speed on the timeline. Show a visual indication when hitting the limit. Key files: useClipResize.ts (main resize logic), timeline-data.ts (getSourceTime), config-utils.ts (duration calc), TimelineCanvas.tsx (resize events), VisualClip.tsx and AudioTrack.tsx (rendering). The track kind (visual vs audio) determines which behavior applies.
