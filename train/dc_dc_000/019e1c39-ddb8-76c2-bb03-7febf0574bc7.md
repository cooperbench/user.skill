**Verdict** The doc mostly holds up for weight loss and the simple parts of pregnancy, but it breaks first on time-derived cache invalidation and pregnancy anchoring. The schema says `current_state` is prompt-ready, yet stale flags, gestational age, rate windows, and denormalized scheduled-task milestones all change without journey-table mutations. Also, the pregnancy example models both `lmp_date` and `due_date` but computes gestational week from LMP, which becomes wrong or inconsistent after a dating-scan correction unless the doc defines a canonical gestational anchor.
