Clip-level settings OVERRIDE track settings, they don't stack on top. Specifically: 1. SCALE: Track scale=0.95 means 'by default, show clips at 95% zoom'. If a clip has its own scale/size override, use THAT instead, not track_scale * clip_scale. The clip override replaces the track default. 2. POSITION: Track scale affects where content appears (centered, scaled down). FIT: Track fit=contain means 'by default, letterbox clips'. If a clip has explicit width/height, use those instead.
