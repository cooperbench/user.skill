And what about goals, you know? How should that work into it? Is that progress markers?

> AGENT

Goals are related to progress markers, but they shouldn’t be the same thing. A clean model would be: `Goal` = what the user is trying to change. `Progress marker` = evidence that shows whether things are moving. Examples: - Goal: “Get back to training 3x/week.” Progress markers: workout count per week, missed-session reasons, streaks. - Goal: “Lose the beer gut.” Progress markers: waist measurement, weight trend, photos if user wants, takeout/alcohol frequency. - Goal: “Feel less winded playing with the kids.” Progress markers: cardio sessions, walk/run time, perceived breathlessness, resting heart rate if available. - Goal: “Bench 225.” Progress markers: bench sets/reps/weight, estimated 1RM, recent PRs. - Goal: “Eat like an adult most weekdays.” Progress markers: protein at meals, takeout nights, alcohol, meal prep, vegetable/fiber notes. I’d make goals first-class, not just inferred from logs. Something like: - `fitness_goals` - `id` - `user_id` - `status`: `active`, `paused`, `completed`, `dropped` - `category`: `strength`, `fat_loss`, `consistency`, `cardio`, `nutrition`, `mobility`, `health` - `plain_language_goal`: “be less winded at soccer with the kids” - `target`: optional structured JSON - `started_at` - `target_date` - `why_it_matters`: optional, user’s own words - `created_by_bot_id` Then progress markers attach to goals, directly or indirectly: - Workout logs can support a consistency or strength goal. - Nutrition logs can support a fat-loss or health goal. - Body measurements can support fat-loss/body-composition goals. - Subjective check-ins can support energy, confidence, soreness, or “feels easier” goals. The bot’s job should be to keep goals human-sized. Not “optimize body composition”; more like: “You said you wanted your belt to fit better and to stop feeling wiped after stairs. We’re watching waist, two lifts a week, and takeout nights.” So yes: progress markers are how goals become trackable. But goals are the organizing layer. They tell the bot which markers matter and prevent it from tracking random fitness data forever.
