One orchestrator, polymorphic over modality. Kill the hardcoded `(iteration shape × style → visual mode)` table. Replace it with a **modality renderer registry** plus **artifact capability declarations**. The table becomes a fallback for the trivial case (image-grid threads) and a tiebreaker, not the spine. ## The primitive: ModalityRenderer A `ModalityRenderer` is a pure descriptor — a tiny manifest, not code-the-agent-writes.
