`--profile` is parsed at [megaplan/cli.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/cli.py:1858). `handle_init()` immediately calls `apply_profile_expansion(args, project_dir)` at [megaplan/handlers/init.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/handlers/init.py:33), then persists the expanded `args.phase_model` into state at [megaplan/handlers/init.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/handlers/init.py:178). Worker routing checks `--phase-model` first at [megaplan/workers.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/workers.py:2359), and only falls back to `~/.config/megaplan/config.json` `agents.*` at [megaplan/workers.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/workers.py:2381). So, if `all-codex` was expanded into `phase_model`, `finalize=codex` should beat `agents.finalize=claude`.

The merge logic is in `apply_profile_expansion()` at [megaplan/profiles/__init__.py](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:387). Its stated precedence is `live CLI > persisted CLI > profile`: comments at lines [395-399](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:395) and [487-493](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:487). It loads the named profile at lines [405-414](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:405), appends profile entries unless an explicit CLI `--phase-model` already covers that phase at lines [476-484](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:476), then rehydrates persisted `state.config.phase_model` for auto subprocesses at lines [494-513](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/megaplan/megaplan/profiles/__init__.py:494).

It ignores profiles entirely. That output can say `finalize=claude` while a run with `--profile all-codex` should still dispatch `finalize` to Codex.
