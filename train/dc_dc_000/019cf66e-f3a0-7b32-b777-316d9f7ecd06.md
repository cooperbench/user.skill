1. The plan is not safe to start from the current repo state. [quiet-finding-reddy.md](/Users/user_c042661f/.claude/plans/quiet-finding-reddy.md#L24) says to branch from `main`, but the repo is currently on `codex/pr19-layered-port` with a large dirty worktree, and [quiet-finding-reddy.md](/Users/user_c042661f/.claude/plans/quiet-finding-reddy.md#L25) only records a commit hash.
