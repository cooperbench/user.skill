> DEVELOPER

When i drag an item to a point should the play head move tehre?

> AGENT

Usually, **yes, after the drop**, not continuously during the drag. For this editor context I’d make it behave like this: - While dragging: show the insertion/ghost position, but don’t move playback or scrub the preview. - On drop: select the moved/inserted clip and move the playhead to the clip’s new start time. - Also center or reveal that clip in the timeline viewport, same as insert/replace. - If the drag is cancelled: leave the playhead where it was. Reason: the playhead is the user’s “current inspection point.” Once they place something, they almost always want to inspect that exact placement. But moving it during drag can feel noisy and can accidentally scrub video/audio while they’re just positioning. For edge cases: - Dragging a clip duration edge: move playhead to the edited edge only after release, maybe start edge for trim-left and end edge for trim-right. - Multi-select drag: move playhead to the earliest selected clip start after drop. - Holding a modifier could preserve current playhead later, but I wouldn’t add that unless users ask.

> DEVELOPER

For multiple clips, where they land?

> AGENT

For multiple clips, move the playhead to the **earliest start time of the moved group after drop**. So if you drag 3 selected clips and they land at: - clip A: `12.0s` - clip B: `14.5s` - clip C: `18.0s` the playhead should go to `12.0s`. That gives the user the beginning of the newly placed arrangement, not an arbitrary individual clip. Also select/keep selected all moved clips, and center the viewport around the group’s start or bounding range.
