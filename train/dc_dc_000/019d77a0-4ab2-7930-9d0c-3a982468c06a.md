> AGENT

I didn’t reproduce it interactively in a browser here, but the code path is pretty clear and there was a real regression.

- The likely cause was in [AudioTrack.tsx](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/src/tools/video-editor/compositions/AudioTrack.tsx#L34). On April 9, 2026, preview audio started forcing `pauseWhenBuffering={true}`. Remotion’s own default for preview audio is `false` in [Audio.js](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/node_modules/remotion/dist/cjs/audio/Audio.js#L81) and [Audio.js](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/node_modules/remotion/dist/cjs/audio/Audio.js#L87).
- When that flag is true, Remotion turns a transient `waiting` event into player-wide buffering, and other media tags get paused as part of sync handling. That behavior is visible in [use-media-playback.js](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/node_modules/remotion/dist/cjs/use-media-playback.js#L61) and [use-media-playback.js](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/node_modules/remotion/dist/cjs/use-media-playback.js#L108).
- Audio clips also were not premounted, so each new audio clip could start cold exactly on its first audible frame. That makes clip boundaries and small network hiccups feel “random”.

I changed [AudioTrack.tsx](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/src/tools/video-editor/compositions/AudioTrack.tsx#L34) to premount audio for `fps` frames and keep preview audio non-blocking with `pauseWhenBuffering={false}`. I also added regression coverage in [AudioTrack.test.tsx](/Users/user_c042661f/Documents/reigh-workspace/reigh-app/src/tools/video-editor/compositions/AudioTrack.test.tsx#L78).
