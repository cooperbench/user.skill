> "Hi {name}, this week we had {N} conversations and touched on {M} ongoing things. Want to talk through anything? Just ask."

Two things make it feel mechanical:
1. **It's a stat dump, not a reflection.** Just counts of conversations + ongoing items, no actual content from the week.
2. **It bypasses the agent.** Compare to `handle_checkin` (line 107) — that one calls `run_agentic_job` when freeform is allowed and only falls back to the template when it has to. Weekly summary always sends the canned string, even inside the 24-hour freeform window where the LLM could write something real.

The fix pattern is already in the codebase: gate on `_can_send_freeform` and route to `run_agentic_job` with a `weekly_summary` kind, letting the agent pull from recent topic state instead of just counting rows. Want me to draft that?
