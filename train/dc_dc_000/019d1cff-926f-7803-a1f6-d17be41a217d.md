        # Implementation Plan: Fix Inconsistent Card Text Truncation

## Overview
Cards on the homepage are 400px-tall flex columns (340px mobile). Each card type renders different internal elements (image, title, text, linkRow) and a `::after` pseudo-element provides a 5em fade gradient at the bottom. The user reports that some cards (e.g. weight/data card) fade nicely while others (e.g. "Second Renaissance plan", "Road to nowhere") cut off too early — meaning their text doesn't fill to the card's bottom edge.

**Root cause identified:** The `.linkRow` element is visually hidden when collapsed (`opacity: 0; pointer-events: none`) but still participates in flex layout — it has `display: inline-block`, `margin-top: 0.5rem`, and content height (~1.5-2rem total). Since `.textContent` has `flex: 1` in a flex column, it fills remaining space *minus* the linkRow's layout footprint. This steals ~1.5-2rem from every card that has a linkRow, causing text to end higher than the fade gradient expects.

Secondary factors:
- Title length varies (1-line vs 2-line titles take different amounts of space)
- The DataCard (WeightsChart) uses a completely different structure (`.textBlock` instead of `.textContent`, no linkRow) which happens to look correct
- `<br />` separators in `ParagraphList` between paragraphs add extra vertical spacing

## Step 1: Make `.linkRow` take zero layout space when collapsed (`Cards.module.css:175-183`)
**Scope:** Small — CSS-only change, highest impact
1. **Modify** the `.linkRow` rule at `src/components/home/Cards.module.css:175` to collapse its layout footprint when not expanded. Change from `display: inline-block` + `opacity: 0` to `height: 0; overflow: hidden; margin: 0; opacity: 0; pointer-events: none`. This removes it from the flex layout calculation while preserving the DOM element for the expand transition.
2. **Update** the `:global(.card.expanded) .linkRow` rule at `Cards.module.css:185` to restore layout: `height: auto; overflow: visible; margin-top: 0.5rem; opacity: 1; pointer-events: auto; transform: translateY(0)`.
3. **Verify** visually that all cards with linkRows (road-to-nowhere, open-source-ai-art, second-renaissance-post, second-renaissance-plan, steerable-motion, sound-of-the-tires) now have text filling to the bottom consistently, and that the linkRow still animates in smoothly when the card is expanded.

## Step 2: Audit remaining per-card structural differences
**Scope:** Small — verify no further fixes needed

3. **Check** whether the DataCard (WeightsChart) text now looks inconsistent compared to the fixed cards. The WeightsChart renders its own `h3` and `.textBlock` outside of the standard `.textContent` pattern. If the fade looks different after Step 1, consider wrapping `.textBlock` content in a `.textContent` div or adding `flex: 1` to `.textBlock`.
