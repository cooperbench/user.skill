What's the root of this problem?

> AGENT

The actual root cause is a mismatch between the tool and the workload, compounded by no process detachment. **The mismatch**: `Bash run_in_background` and sub-agents are designed for short-lived background work within a turn — "kick off a build, get notified when it finishes." Megaplan runs for 30 minutes to hours. The harness reclaims those background slots on time/concurrency limits, and when a slot is reclaimed, the shell it owned gets killed. **The compounding failure**: nothing in the launch command detached the megaplan process from its parent shell. The process tree was `harness → zsh (background task) → megaplan init → megaplan plan → claude opus`. When the harness killed the zsh, SIGHUP propagated down the whole tree because megaplan wasn't in its own session. A single `setsid` or `nohup … & disown` at the launch site would have isolated megaplan from the parent's death — the harness could have reaped the zsh and megaplan would have kept running. Either fix alone would have saved it. Together they were a guaranteed death: - Right tool, no detachment → eventually slot reclaim kills everything. - Wrong tool, with detachment → megaplan survives the slot reclaim, finishes on its own. - Right tool + detachment → robust.
