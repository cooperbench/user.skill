        # Implementation Plan: Sprint 9 Control-Rail and Travel-Matrix Parity

## Overview
The gate feedback shows the original plan was pointed at the right sprint surface, but it understated the root cause: Section 3A is not just missing fixture coverage. The current dimensional route schema collapses control modes because `travel_guidance.kind` is used without the mode, so WAN VACE flow/canny/depth/raw and LTX control video/pose/depth/canny/cameraman cannot carry independent support, selector, or blocking decisions. Sprint 9 therefore needs a mode-aware route/support contract before promotion.

The implementation remains scoped to the original goal: route parity, control guidance, continuity, media post-processing, matrix smoke reporting, and replay. The revision makes four corrections mandatory: use a single executable Section 3A source synchronized with `docs/migration-vibecomfy.md`, audit/update all active route derivation implementations including `reigh-app`, preserve thumbnail semantics in the new lifecycle completion path, and replay full route snapshot contracts rather than route keys only.
