> AGENT

The cleanest fix is to treat LoRAs the same as prompts in the inheritance cascade — explicitly zero them out for new shots at `shotSettingsInheritance.ts:163` and move "sticky LoRA" behavior behind an explicit project-level default (either `projects.settings.defaultLoras` or just re-enabling `handleLoadProjectLoras` with `disableAutoLoad: false`).
