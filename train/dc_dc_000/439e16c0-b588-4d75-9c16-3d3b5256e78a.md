## How New Items Get Ordered in the Plan Queue

**TL;DR:** When a scan adds new findings that weren't in the plan before, they go **to the end of the queue, after all manually-ordered items**, but **maintain their mechanical sort order** (by tier, confidence, count, etc.) among themselves.

### The Ordering Flow:

1. **Initial Build (`build_work_queue()` in `core.py`)**
   - All findings are built and sorted via `item_sort_key()` (lines 342-343 in core.py)
   - Sort order is: **tier** (ascending) → **confidence** (ascending) → **count** (descending) → ID
   - Subjective items forced to T4

2. **Plan Application (`_apply_plan_order()` in core.py, lines 100-186)**
   - The plan's `queue_order` list acts as a **fixed prefix** (line 163: `result = ordered + remaining`)
   - **Step 1:** Items in `queue_order` appear first, **in that exact order** (lines 146-149)
   - **Step 2:** Items NOT in `queue_order` (the "remaining" list) keep their **mechanical sort** (line 160)
   - **Step 3:** Skipped items append last (line 165)

3. **Reconciliation (`reconcile_plan_after_scan()` in `reconcile.py`)**
   - **Only removes** dead findings from the plan (supersedes them)
   - Does **NOT add** new findings to `queue_order`
   - New items remain in the "remaining" bucket, sorted mechanically

### Key Detail:

**New findings are NOT automatically added to `queue_order`.**  They appear after ordered items but are sorted internally by their tier/confidence/count. So if you scan and find a new T1 item, it will appear:

```
[manually-ordered items in plan.queue_order]
[NEW T1 item]  ← here (sorted with other unordered items)
[other unordered T2, T3, T4 items]
[skipped items]
```

The queue respects user-managed order (via `queue_order`) but doesn't force new discoveries into that queue — they go to the mechanical sort tier instead.
