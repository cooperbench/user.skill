The smallest end-to-end path is to extend that system rather than introduce a parallel scheduler: add a new `scheduled_task` job type, store the agent-created brief and recurrence in `scheduled_jobs.context`, add agent tools for create/list/update/cancel, and add a handler that calls `run_agentic_job(user, trigger_metadata)` with `kind="scheduled_task"` and no inbound messages.
