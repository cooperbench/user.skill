### `remote-artifact` adapter — full implementation lands here

Schema reservation existed in S3 (the stub at `astrid/core/adapter/remote_artifact.py` rejects with the deferral message). Now it becomes real.

**The adapter owns:**
- **Dispatch** — submit the remote job (the executor pack handles the actual API calls; the adapter is the framework wrapper).
- **Status polling** — periodic check of remote job state.
- **Artifact fetch** — pull outputs into the canonical `produces/` path on completion.
- **Checksum verification** — sha256 each fetched artifact; compare against the remote-declared checksum if provided. Mismatch → `check_status: mismatch` + step fails.
- **Partial-failure semantics** — if 3 of 5 artifacts arrive cleanly and 2 are missing/corrupt, the step is in `awaiting-fetch` with the missing/corrupt items enumerable.

**Step states added:**
- `awaiting-fetch` — remote job done, but at least one artifact is missing or failed checksum. Step does NOT complete; cursor does NOT advance.

**New verb:**
- `astrid step retry-fetch <step-id>` — re-attempt fetch + checksum for a step in `awaiting-fetch`. Idempotent. Promotes step to `completed` if all artifacts now resolve.

**Completion contract:** "remote done AND artifacts pulled AND checksums match." Anything less leaves the step in `awaiting-fetch`.
