**Recommended Shape**

Use three layers:

- `ModelSpec`: static facts only. `id`, `workerModelName`, `frameStep`, `fps`, `defaultFrames`, `defaultSteps`, `supportedGuidanceModes`, `supportedContinuationStrategies`, `uiCapabilities`.
- `GenerationIntent`: user choices. `selectedModel`, `smoothContinuations`, `travelGuidance`, `hasStructureVideo`, `ltxHdResolution`.
- `ResolvedGenerationPolicy`: derived runtime behavior. `travelMode`, `continuation`, `guidePipeline`, `maskPolicy`, `frameLimits`, `requestFields`.

That gives you one place on the client and one place on the worker to answer “what do we do for this run?”, without pretending that runtime policy is a model capability.

- Make an explicit decision for distilled LTX: either `smooth continuation` and `ltx_control` are mutually exclusive in the UI, or add a merged guide composer on the worker.
- Treat `continuation_config` as canonical and derive `frame_overlap` from it in one boundary layer only.
- Add a state/persistence section for “frame count when policy changes” so toggling SC or switching models is reversible and predictable.
